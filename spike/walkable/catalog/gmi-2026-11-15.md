## What matters for walkable worlds

- 3D: only `hunyuan-3d-pro` ($0.375 per model). No panorama, depth, splat, scene-reconstruction or HunyuanWorld model.
- Real-time: `Krea-Realtime-14B` streams video from text, images or video ($0.025 per second) but takes no movement or camera controls, so it isn't a walkable world model. No lingbot, abot, eden or Genie-style world model.
- Panoramas have to come from general image models prompted for a 2:1 equirectangular image.
